    Before you ask

    Questions we hear from distributors.

    Our sales reps work on paper and WhatsApp.

    Normal. Orders come in on WhatsApp, delivery notes are signed on paper and the invoicing system gets it later. Radar reads all three, and it tells you when they disagree.

    We use Pixel, Dolphin, an old system or Excel.

    If it can export a file or has a screen, we have a way in. Most of our connections in Lebanon start from an export and an invoice folder.

    We invoice in dollars and in lira.

    Radar keeps both, converts at the rate you set, and never mixes them. The morning brief shows you cash in each currency.

    We have 400 customers.

    That is why you cannot rank them yourself every morning. Radar ranks every one of them by how late they are and how they usually pay.

    Can it hold a delivery on its own?

    No. It never acts on a customer without you. It tells you Mansour is 52 days late and the van leaves at 06:00, and you decide.

    Our drivers and reps will not use a new app.

    They do not have to. They keep working the way they work. Only you, and the people you choose, get the messages.

    Does it work in Arabic?

    Yes. Arabic and English in the same conversation. Your team writes the way they already write.

    Where does our data go?

    Nowhere. It stays in your accounts. We only read it, and we sign an NDA before the audit if you want one.

    How fast do we see something real?

    Two to three weeks to version one, on your own invoices and delivery notes. The late-payer list usually starts in the first week.
