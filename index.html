Before you ask

The questions every owner asks.

Our data is a mess.

Everybody starts there. Most of the first build is putting one number in one place and agreeing what it means. Clean nothing before we talk.

Where does our data live?

In your accounts. We read only, unless you ask us to write. We sign an NDA before the audit if you want one.

Do we need someone technical?

No. If your team uses WhatsApp, they can use this. Nobody logs into anything new.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic and English in the same conversation. Your staff write the way they already write.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every number shows its source. When it is not sure it says so, and it never messages a customer or supplier unless you set that rule.

We are a small company.

Good. Small companies feel the hours come back faster, because the owner does the collecting. Three people or sixty, it fits.

How fast do we see something real?

Two to three weeks to version one, running on your own data. Not a slide deck.

What about power cuts and internet?

It runs in the cloud and reaches you on WhatsApp, so it works while your office does not. If a system goes dark, it tells you instead of inventing numbers.
