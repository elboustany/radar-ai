Before you ask

Questions we hear from restaurant owners.

Our POS is old and has no export.

That is most restaurants in Lebanon. If it prints a Z report or has a screen, we have a way in. We connect to Omega, Foodics, Ebs and the local systems, and to plain Excel.

We do not have recipe costs.

Almost nobody does. In the first two weeks we cost your top 30 dishes with your chef, from your real invoices. After that Radar keeps them up to date every night.

The chef will not fill in a sheet.

Good, because there is no sheet. Radar reads the POS and the invoices. The chef gets a message on his phone in the morning, in Arabic if he prefers.

We are a cash business.

Radar reads the POS and the bank, and it tells you when the two do not match. Cash is where the gaps hide, and that is where it looks first.

The delivery apps hide our data.

They give you a settlement report, and it usually says less than your orders. Radar reads both and shows you the difference, platform by platform.

We have three branches on two different systems.

Normal. Radar puts every branch on the same numbers so you can compare them, and the branch managers each get their own morning message.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic and English in the same conversation. Your staff write the way they already write.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere. It stays in your accounts. We only read it, and we sign an NDA before the audit if you want one.

How fast do we see something real?

Two to three weeks to version one, on your own tickets and invoices. The 07:00 message usually starts in the first week.
